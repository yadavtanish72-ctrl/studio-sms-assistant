# What to Bring

## Essentials for Any Class

Bring a water bottle and a small towel to any class at Northline Studio. There is a
filtered water refill station by the front desk. Wear whatever you can move in; fitted
clothing is easier for instructors to cue in Reformer Pilates and Barre Basics. Bring a
padlock if you plan to use a day-use locker, since the studio does not supply them. Phones
must be silenced and are not permitted on the studio floor during class.

## Grip Socks

Grip socks are required for Reformer Pilates and Barre Basics and you will not be allowed
to participate in those two classes without them. They are not required for any other
class on the Northline schedule, though some clients wear them to Restore & Stretch by
preference. Grip socks are sold at the front desk for $18 in standard adult sizes. Bare
feet and regular socks are not permitted on the reformers or at the barre.

## Mats and Equipment Rental

Mats are available to borrow at every class. Mat rental is free for Essential, Plus, and
Unlimited members and free for intro-offer clients, and costs $3 per class for drop-in
and class-pack clients. All other equipment used in class, including blocks, straps,
weights, and kettlebells, is provided free to everyone. Northline does not rent grip
socks, so those must be bought or brought.
