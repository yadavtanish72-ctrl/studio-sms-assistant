# Studio Policies

## Cancelling a Class

Northline Studio requires at least 12 hours notice to cancel a booked class. Cancel 12 or
more hours before the class start time and your credit is returned in full with no
charge, or your monthly class allotment is restored if you are on a membership. Cancel
with less than 12 hours notice and the class is treated as a late cancellation: class
pack and drop-in clients forfeit the credit, and Essential, Plus, and Unlimited members
lose one class from that month's allotment.

## No-Shows

A no-show is a booked class you neither attend nor cancel. No-shows are penalized more
heavily than late cancellations at Northline Studio. Class pack and drop-in clients
forfeit the credit, and members lose one class from that month's allotment, exactly as
with a late cancel. In addition, every no-show carries a $15 fee charged to the card on
file. Three no-shows within a rolling 60-day period result in booking privileges being
suspended for two weeks.

## Late-Cancellation Waivers

Plus and Unlimited members receive late-cancellation waivers that erase the penalty for a
late cancel. Plus members get 2 waivers per calendar year and Unlimited members get 4 per
calendar year. Waivers apply only to late cancellations and never to no-shows. Essential
members, class pack clients, drop-in clients, and intro-offer clients receive no waivers
at all. Unused waivers do not carry over into the next calendar year and are applied
automatically to the first eligible late cancel.

## Membership Freezes

Plus and Unlimited members may freeze their membership. Plus members can freeze for up to
1 month per calendar year, and Unlimited members can freeze for up to 2 months per
calendar year, in whole-month blocks requested at least 7 days before the next billing
date. Essential memberships cannot be frozen under the standard policy. Separately, a
documented medical freeze of up to 3 months is available on any membership tier,
including Essential, with a note from a healthcare provider.

## Waitlists

When a class is full you can join its waitlist at no charge. Northline auto-enrolls
waitlisted clients as spots open, up to 1 hour before the class start time, and notifies
you by text. If you are auto-enrolled from the waitlist you are booked into the class and
the standard 12-hour cancellation policy no longer protects you, so decline promptly if
you can no longer attend. After the 1-hour cutoff the waitlist closes and no further
enrollments are made.
