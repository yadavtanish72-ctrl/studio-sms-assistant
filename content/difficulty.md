# Class Difficulty Levels

## Level 1 — Foundations

Level 1 classes at Northline Studio are built for beginners and for anyone easing back
into training. Instructors teach every movement from the ground up, cue modifications
throughout, and never assume prior experience. No fitness baseline is required. The Level
1 classes on the Northline schedule are Sunrise Flow, Barre Basics, and Restore &
Stretch.

## Level 2 — Intermediate

Level 2 classes assume you are comfortable with basic movement patterns and can sustain
moderate effort for the full session. Instructors cue faster and demonstrate less, though
modifications are still offered on request. Northline recommends about a month of regular
practice before starting Level 2. The Level 2 classes on the Northline schedule are
Vinyasa Flow, Strength Circuit, and Reformer Pilates.

## Level 3 — Advanced

Level 3 classes are the hardest at Northline Studio and assume an established training
base, good body awareness, and the ability to self-modify without cueing. These classes
move at pace with minimal rest and are not appropriate for people new to training or
returning from injury without clearance. The Level 3 classes on the Northline schedule
are Power Yoga and Express HIIT.

## Choosing a Level

If you are unsure which level suits you, Northline recommends starting at Level 1 and
moving up once you can complete a class without needing modifications. Levels describe
intensity and pace, not fitness or ability, and moving between them freely is expected.
Nothing prevents a client from booking any level, including on the intro offer, but
instructors may suggest an easier class if a Level 3 session looks like a poor fit.
