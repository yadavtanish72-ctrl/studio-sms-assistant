# Location, Parking and Facilities

## Parking

Northline Studio has a free lot behind the building with 14 spaces, available
first-come-first-served and entered from the alley off Bell Street. Street parking on
Northline Avenue is metered at $2 per hour from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Monday through
Saturday, and is free after 6:00 PM and all day Sunday. There is a bike rack directly out
front; bikes cannot be brought inside the studio.

## Getting In and Arrival

Northline Studio is at 1420 Northline Avenue. Doors open 20 minutes before the first
class of the day and the front desk is staffed until the last class begins. Please arrive
at least 10 minutes early if it is your first visit, so there is time to check in and get
set up. The studio door is locked 5 minutes after each class starts and late arrivals
cannot be admitted, which counts as a no-show under the studio's booking policy.

## Lockers and Showers

Cubbies for bags and shoes are free and sit just inside the studio entrance. Day-use
lockers are available at no charge but you must bring your own padlock, and lockers are
cleared nightly with contents moved to lost and found. There are two showers, and clean
towels are provided free of charge at the front desk. Nothing may be left at the studio
overnight.
