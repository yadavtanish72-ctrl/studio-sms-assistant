# Pricing & Memberships

## Drop-In and Class Packs

A single drop-in class at Northline Studio is $28, bookable without a membership. Class
packs are cheaper per class and do not auto-renew. A 5-class pack is $125 and expires
three months after purchase. A 10-class pack is $230 and expires six months after
purchase. Pack credits are non-transferable and cannot be shared between people. Packs
and memberships cannot be combined on the same booking.

## Membership Tiers

Northline offers three monthly memberships, all month-to-month with no long-term
contract. Essential is $89 per month and includes 8 classes per month. Plus is $139 per
month and includes 16 classes per month. Unlimited is $179 per month with no cap on
classes. Unused classes on Essential and Plus do not roll over to the following month.
Cancelling a membership requires 30 days notice; billing stops after the next cycle.

## What Each Membership Includes

All three membership tiers include free mat rental at every class. Guest passes differ by
tier: Essential includes no guest passes, Plus includes 2 guest passes per calendar year,
and Unlimited includes 4 guest passes per calendar year. Membership freezes are available
on Plus and Unlimited only; Essential memberships cannot be frozen except under the
medical exception. Drop-in and class-pack clients do not receive mat rental or guest
passes.
