# Trainers

## Maya Chen

Maya Chen teaches Sunrise Flow, Vinyasa Flow, and Restore & Stretch at Northline Studio.
She is an RYT-500 registered yoga teacher with eleven years of teaching experience and
trained in Mysore, India. Her Sunrise Flow classes are deliberately gentle and built for
people new to yoga, while her Vinyasa Flow classes move faster and assume some prior
practice. Maya also runs the studio's teacher mentorship program. She teaches on Monday,
Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday.

## Dev Okonkwo

Dev Okonkwo teaches Express HIIT and Strength Circuit at Northline Studio. He is a
NASM-certified personal trainer and a former collegiate 400m runner, and he specializes
in conditioning for people with limited time to train. His Express HIIT classes are 30
minutes and rated Level 3 Advanced; his Strength Circuit classes are 50 minutes and rated
Level 2 Intermediate. Dev teaches on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Saturday.

## Kai Reyes

Kai Reyes teaches Power Yoga and Strength Circuit at Northline Studio. Kai holds an
RYT-200 yoga certification alongside a StrongFirst kettlebell instructor credential, and
brings a strength-training approach to yoga. Kai's Power Yoga classes are 75 minutes and
rated Level 3 Advanced, making them the longest and hardest classes on the Northline
schedule. Kai teaches on Monday, Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday.

## Sofia Marchetti

Sofia Marchetti teaches Reformer Pilates, Barre Basics, and Restore & Stretch at
Northline Studio. She completed a comprehensive 600-hour Pilates certification and spent
six years as a professional contemporary dancer before teaching full time. Sofia works
frequently with clients returning from injury and is the person to ask about
modifications. She teaches on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Saturday, and Sunday.
