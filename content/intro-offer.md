# Intro Offer

## What You Get

The Northline intro offer is $49 for two weeks of unlimited classes. It covers every
class on the schedule with no level restriction and includes free mat rental for the
duration. The two-week window starts on the date of your first booked class, not the date
you purchase it, so buying early does not shorten the offer.

## Who Is Eligible

The intro offer is available to first-time Northline clients only, one per person, ever.
You must be 18 or older and present photo ID with a local address at your first class.
Clients who have previously held any Northline membership, class pack, or drop-in booking
are not eligible. The offer cannot be gifted or transferred to another person.

## Terms and Restrictions

The intro offer cannot be combined with class packs, guest passes, or any other
promotion. Intro clients receive no late-cancellation waivers during the two weeks, so
every late cancel and no-show counts against them under the standard studio policy.
Intro-offer clients cannot freeze or pause the two-week window for any reason other than
a documented medical exception. At the end of two weeks the offer simply expires; it does
not auto-convert into a paid membership.
